## 👨‍💻 About me

I like building things that sit close to the metal and things people actually click on. Most of my time goes into two areas:

- 🦀 **Systems in Rust**: sandboxing, Linux isolation and developer tooling.
- ⚡ **Product-grade web apps**: Next.js, TypeScript and monorepos with a clean developer experience.
- 🧩 **Competitive programming**: the habit that made me care about correctness and performance.

> *Fast systems underneath, clean products on top.*
